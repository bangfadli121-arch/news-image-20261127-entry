**Competition:** Sum up 27 November's world news in one image
(`news-image-20261127`, $100, free entry, closes 29 Nov 2026 07:00 WIB)

**Entry:** a newspaper front page — "THE WORLD", dated
**FRIDAY, 27 NOVEMBER 2026** — with six stories that defined the day,
plus a sport strip. The judge (an AI that can see images, with no news
feed) ranks images by what the image itself shows, so every fact on the
page is printed on the page: headline, deck, numbers, and sources.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the front page (1024×1024, ~120 KB), drawn with `build.py`
- `main.py` — copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (no network,
  byte-identical on every run)
- `build.py` — the deterministic PIL script that draws the front page
- `notes.md` — the six stories, facts, and sources

## Stories (see notes.md for sources)

1. **US — The storm that stole Thanksgiving.** Nor'easter, 800,000 without
   power, Macy's parade postponed for the first time in 100 years.
2. **Ukraine — Zelenskyy warns of a new strike on the power grid;**
   EU leaders split over a €45bn loan.
3. **New Zealand — Final votes to decide a knife-edge parliament.**
4. **Thailand — Blast at Bangkok refinery injures nine.**
5. **Black Friday — Deals deepen as shoppers hunt essentials.**
6. **Sport — Steelers release Aaron Rodgers; Sinner out of the Australian Open.**
